Designated Marksman Rifle: MK-14 EBR

> WEAPON: MK-14 Enhanced Battle Rifle (EBR)

> ORIGIN: United States

> CALIBER: 7.62×51mm

> ROLE: Designated Marksman / CQC

> STATUS: OPERATIONAL

> TAC. DESC.: The MK-14 Enhanced Battle Rifle (EBR), one of the U.S. military's most beloved 'failed' battle rifles, is a selective-fire military marksman rifle chambered in 7.62x51mm NATO. It was designed to bridge the gap between a standard infantry rifle and a long-range sniper rifle, giving squads a heavy-hitting option that works in tight urban spaces and open fields alike.

> VETERAN. DESC.: "It’s got some recoil compared to the M110... suppressors are for women, children, and the Swedes. Let it roar." — traffic_cone_no54 (via Reddit/YouTube community) "It would melt anybody in anything." (Source: Reddit user ImperialSupplies)

Assault Rifle: HK-416

> WEAPON: H416 [Real name: HK-416] (Assault Rifle)

> ORIGIN: Germany

> CALIBER: 5.56×45mm

> ROLE: Assault Rifle / CQC

> STATUS: OPERATIONAL

> TAC. DESC.: The Heckler & Koch HK416 is an assault rifle chambered in 5.56x45mm NATO. It was specifically designed to fix the reliability issues of the M4/M16 platform. While it looks like an M4, its bolt is actually borrowed from the G36, making it one of the most dependable rifles ever made.

> VETERAN. DESC.: "It’s overwhelmingly strong but it has no character. It just beams." —(A disgruntled pro on Reddit) "I took a 650k build... pointed the red dot at him and just let it go. He was completely filled in about one second." —(Describing a high-speed elimination)

Tryhard's Battle Rifle: FN FAL

> WEAPON: FN FAL (Assault Rifle)

> ORIGIN: Belgium

> CALIBER: 7.62×51mm

> ROLE: Battle Rifle / Mid Range (But the player use it with a shortened barrel and three cans of Chinese Beer)

> STATUS: OPERATIONAL (Used by B.O.P.E)

> TAC. DESC.: The FN FAL (Fusil Automatique Léger) is a gas-operated battle rifle chambered in 7.62x51mm NATO. Developed in Belgium during the Cold War, it earned its reputation for being incredibly reliable and simple to maintain, leading to its adoption by over 90 countries especially in the B.O.P.E.

> VETERAN. DESC.: The FN FAL (Feet or Armpit Licker) is a battle rifle in Arena Breakout, it’s basically the MK14’s older, meaner, and slightly less refined grandfather. It's built for one thing: throwing 7.62x51mm rounds downrange until the other guy stops moving while making sure the barrel goes rising to the moon.

Rat's SMG: MP5

> WEAPON: MP5 (SMG)

> ORIGIN: Germany

> CALIBER: 9×19mm

> ROLE: SMG / Close Range

> STATUS: OPERATIONAL ("Who dares wins.")

> TAC. DESC.: The Heckler & Koch MP5 is a 9x19mm Parabellum submachine gun developed in the 1960s. While most SMGs of that era were simple "spray and pray" tools, the MP5 was designed like a miniature rifle, making it the go-to choice for elite units like the SAS and Navy SEALs for decades.

> VETERAN. DESC.: The Heckler & Koch MP5 is a 9x19mm submachine gun that became a a meme for those leg lickers out there. In Arena Breakout, it’s the "budget king" that can punch way above its weight class if you know how to tickle the right spots (and by that, I mean the enemy's legs).

> BONUS: This is the only gun in recorded history to be used to mag dump two of them on a terrorist who fell from the stairs.
